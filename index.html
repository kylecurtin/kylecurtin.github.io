№ 01 A Letter

A few words on practice.

My work sits at the intersection of three things that don't usually get talked about together: responsible finance, sustainability strategy, and AI agents. I'm a Senior Associate on the Multi-Strategy team at Malk, part of SLR, where I advise the firm's largest and most complex private-markets clients across the investment lifecycle.

Day to day, I lead investment due diligence on prospective deals, build annual sustainability reporting programs that hold up to LP scrutiny, and run longer-horizon fund advisory engagements — ESG program augmentation, value creation, and the operating muscle that turns commitments into measurable outcomes.

Alongside the client work I lead Malk's internal AI agent development. I build process-improvement agents with our Innovations team and chair the firm's AI committee, where we triage agent ideas from across roles, maintain a development backlog, and share what we're learning so the practice compounds rather than fragmenting team by team.

— K.C.

№ 03 The Studio

On building
the agents that
build the work.

I lead the agent practice at Malk in two roles — one builder, one steward.

As lead agent developer, I partner with our Innovations team to build internal agents that take work off our consultants' plates: research, drafting, review, synthesis. The goal isn't novelty. It's leverage on the actual bottlenecks we hit inside DD, reporting, and advisory.

As chair of Malk's AI committee, I bring people together from across verticals and roles to triage agent ideas, prioritize the build backlog, and share what's working (and what isn't) — so the practice compounds rather than fragmenting team by team.

From the desk

“The most useful agents we build are unglamorous. They're the second pair of eyes on a 200-page diligence pack at 9 p.m.”
