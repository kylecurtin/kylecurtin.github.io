About

Finance, sustainability, and the agents that connect them.

I sit on the Multi-Strategy team within Responsible Finance at SLR (formerly Malk Partners), where I advise private markets clients across the investment lifecycle. My day-to-day spans diligence on prospective investments, building annual reporting programs for funds and their portfolios, and longer-horizon fund-advisory work — ESG program augmentation, value creation, and the operating muscle that turns sustainability commitments into measurable outcomes.

Alongside the client work, I lead our internal AI agent development. I build process-improvement agents with the Innovations team and chair the firm's AI committee, where we triage agent ideas from across roles and verticals, maintain the development backlog, and share AI practices firm-wide.

What I Do

Three lines of client work.

Investment Due Diligence

ESG and sustainability diligence on prospective investments for multi-strategy private markets clients — surfacing risk, opportunity, and the operating posture of management teams.

Annual Reporting

Building and refining annual sustainability and ESG reporting programs that hold up to LP scrutiny — and that funds can actually run year over year.

Fund Advisory

Longer-horizon engagements: ESG program augmentation, value creation roadmaps, and the firm-level infrastructure to make responsible-finance commitments stick.

AI Leadership

Lead agent developer. AI committee chair.

As the lead agent developer at Malk, I partner with our Innovations team to build internal agents that take work off our consultants' plates — research, drafting, review, synthesis. The goal isn't novelty; it's leverage on the actual bottlenecks we hit in DD, reporting, and advisory.

As chair of the firm's AI committee, I bring together people from across verticals and roles to triage agent ideas, prioritize the build backlog, and share what's working (and what isn't) so the practice compounds rather than fragmenting team by team.
